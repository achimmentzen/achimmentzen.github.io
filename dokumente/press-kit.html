One-line description

Achim Mentzen turns his own naked body into the material of a live artwork examining nudity, shame, visibility, objectification and bodily self-determination.

50-word version

Achim Mentzen – Living Sculpture / Transparenzprojekt is a live-art project about the social meaning of the naked human body. Its central format is a seven-day continuous performance in which ordinary life becomes part of the artwork. The project examines shame, public visibility, the gaze, objectification and self-determination.

150-word version

Achim Mentzen is developing Living Sculpture / Transparenzprojekt as a public performance project since 2026. The central work is a seven-day continuous live performance in which the artist remains visibly nude within an agreed performance environment. Sleeping, eating, body care, conversation and ordinary activities can become part of the work.

The project begins with a direct proposition: the human body is not obscene. It examines how nudity becomes charged with shame, sexualisation, social rules and expectations. The artist deliberately makes his own body visible and accepts that looking, questioning, evaluation and objectification can become part of the artistic situation.

Living Sculpture is not presented as a sexual service. Safety, consent, house rules, legal requirements and explicit boundaries are part of the production framework.

The project combines performance art with the artist's professional background in business, sales, marketing, quality management and adult education.
