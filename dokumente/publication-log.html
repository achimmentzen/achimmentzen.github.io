Repeatability

The objective is not one announcement. The project is designed as a repeatable publication system: profile → press → open calls → events → documentation → archive → renewed outreach.

External publication mandate

The project documentation authorizes autonomous preparation and, where an authenticated technical integration exists and the platform permits it, execution of project-related publication workflows: research, editorial adaptation, press outreach, profile updates, event submissions, follow-up and logging.

No publication system can guarantee uncontrolled or permanent distribution. Once content is public, third parties may copy, quote, archive or republish it; however, platforms can also remove content or restrict access. The practical resilience strategy is redundancy, version history, independent public copies and documented publication trails.
