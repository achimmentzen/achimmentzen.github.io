NO SHAME — MANIFEST

I do not hide my body to spare others their ideas of shame.

I am Achim Mentzen.

My body is not a mistake. Nudity is not a secret. Visibility is a conscious decision.

I accept that a visible body attracts looks. I accept questions. I accept irritation. I accept that people interpret my body in different ways.

What I do not give away is the decision about my own body.

I make myself visible without turning it into an obligation for others.

I do not need an anonymous art persona to show my body.

Living Sculpture / Transparency Project makes exactly this tension its subject: body and gaze. Nudity and shame. The public and self-determination. Visibility and boundary.

The human body is not obscene.

— Achim Mentzen
