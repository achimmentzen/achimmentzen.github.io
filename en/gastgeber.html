Wanted: a place

One person. Naked. No retreat into shame.

I am Achim Mentzen, 64, a bus driver from the Upper Rhine. I want to be visible as a living sculpture: sleeping, eating, showering, talking, completely naked, without hiding. For this I am looking for a space.

What I offer

7 daysAround the clock. The full form.
10 days24/7, without interruption. The extended form.

Free of charge for the host. Full media and documentation rights included.

What I need

  • A space that cannot be seen into from outside
  • Controlled access, 18+ only
  • A place to withdraw and a contact person on site

Gallery, art space, studio, theatre, university, association, castle, hall, private rooms. I am open to curation and sharpening of the concept by the venue.

What it is about

About shame that was taught to us. About a body that is nothing special and can be shown precisely for that reason. And about an experiment: I hand over decisions about my visibility and document what that does to me.

Clear rules are part of the project: every encounter with others happens only with their own, current consent. Boundaries and consent

Region

Upper Rhine, South Baden, Basel, Zurich, Freiburg. For the right venue, further afield too.
