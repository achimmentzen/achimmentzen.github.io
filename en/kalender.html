Performance calendar · 24/7

Seven days.
Fully visible.

Where a date belongs to Living Sculpture, the calendar describes a performance with continuous bodily presence. The naked body becomes visible, tangible and part of the public artwork.

The core format is designed for 24 hours a day over seven days . Single dates and everyday performances can explore this basic motif in shorter formats.
